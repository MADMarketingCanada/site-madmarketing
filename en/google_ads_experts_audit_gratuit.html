What an audit usually reveals

After more than 20 years managing Google Ads campaigns, we see the same issues come up again and again — usually invisible to anyone not in the account every day.

Wasted budget

Irrelevant keywords, poorly calibrated bids, missing negative keywords: a good chunk of your budget can go to searches that never lead to a sale.

Missing conversion tracking

Without reliable tracking of calls, form fills, or sales, Google's algorithm is optimizing blind — and you don't really know what's working.

Mismatched landing pages

A great ad that leads to a slow, generic, or poorly targeted page loses most of its clicks before they ever get a chance to convert.

Frequently asked questions about the free audit

What is a Google Ads audit?

It's a complete review of your existing campaigns (structure, keywords, bidding, landing pages, conversion tracking) to identify what's working, what's wasting your budget, and what could be improved.

Is it really free and with no obligation?

Yes, completely. No credit card, no contract to sign. You get our analysis and recommendations, and you decide freely what to do next.

Do I need to already have active Google Ads campaigns?

No. If you already have campaigns, we analyze them directly. If you don't yet, the audit becomes a strategic consultation to assess your potential and plan a profitable first launch.

What do I need to provide for the audit?

Simply read-only access to your Google Ads account (or Google Analytics/Merchant Center, if relevant). No sensitive information or passwords are required.

How long does the audit take?

Our analysis typically takes 48 hours. We then walk you through the results in a 30-minute call, with concrete, prioritized recommendations.

Am I required to work with you after the audit?

Not at all. The audit is yours to keep, whether you apply it yourself, with your current team, or with us. We work without contracts: results are what keep us together, not a signature.
