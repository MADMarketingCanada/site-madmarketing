Privacy policy

How MAD Marketing collects, uses and protects your personal information, in accordance with Quebec's Law 25.

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Who we are

MAD Marketing is a family-run web marketing agency based in Quebec. This policy applies to the madmarketing.ca website and describes our personal information practices.

The information we collect

  • What you send us : when you fill out our contact form or book a call, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email, phone number and business name.
  • Browsing data : with your consent, cookies measure site traffic and the performance of our advertising campaigns (pages visited, source, device type).

Why we use it

  • Responding to your requests and preparing your free quote;
  • Planning and holding the appointments you book;
  • Understanding how the site is used so we can improve it;
  • Measuring and optimizing our advertising campaigns.

We never sell your personal information.

Cookies

On your first visit, a banner asks for your consent. Until you accept, no measurement or advertising cookie is set.

  • Essential cookies : required for the site to work, such as remembering your consent choice. Always active.
  • Analytics cookies : measure site traffic (Google Analytics). Set only if you accept.
  • Advertising cookies : measure the performance of our campaigns (Google Ads, Meta). Set only if you accept.

You can change your mind at any time through the “Manage cookies” link at the bottom of every page, or by deleting cookies in your browser settings. Your choice is kept for six months, after which we ask again.

Who we share it with

Your information is processed by service providers that help us operate, such as our client relationship platform (contact form and booking) and, if you consent, Google and Meta measurement services. These providers may store data outside Quebec; we limit shared information to the strict minimum.

Retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, then it is deleted or anonymized. Access is limited to the team members who need it.

Your rights

In accordance with Law 25, you can request access to the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected, withdraw your consent or request its deletion.

Reach us

For any question about this policy or to exercise your rights, contact the person responsible for the protection of personal information at MAD Marketing through our contact form or by phone at 514 995-5671. We respond quickly, as always.
