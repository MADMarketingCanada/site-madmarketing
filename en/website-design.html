Frequently asked questions about website design

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on the size of the site, the number of pages and the features you need (showcase site or online store). We have solutions for every budget: request your free quote, with no obligation, and together we will determine what suits your project.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Yes. All our websites are designed to display perfectly on mobile, tablet and desktop. It's essential, both for your visitors' experience and for your search ranking, since Google favours mobile-friendly sites.

Is SEO included?

Our websites are built on foundations optimized for organic search: clear structure, fast load times, proper tags and well-organized content. To go further, we also offer a dedicated organic SEO service .

Can I sell online with my website?

Absolutely. We build everything from showcase websites to complete online stores, with secure payment and product management. We help you choose the solution best suited to your business.

Will I be able to manage my website myself?

Yes. We design websites you can update easily, and we support you so you become autonomous. If you prefer to delegate, we can also handle maintenance and updates.
