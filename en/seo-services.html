Frequently asked questions about GEO

What is GEO (AI search optimization)?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) means optimizing your content so it is understood and cited by generative AI such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overviews, on top of solid classic SEO in traditional search results.

SEO vs GEO: what's the difference?

SEO optimizes your site for traditional search engines (Google, Bing), where the visitor clicks a link among the results. GEO optimizes your content for generative AI (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews), which often answers directly without sending a click your way. The two complement each other: a site well-structured for SEO is already halfway ready for GEO.

Does Google Ads help my GEO?

Not directly — generative AI doesn't use your ad campaigns to decide who to cite. But a site that performs well in Google Ads often has a clear offer and compelling content, two qualities that also help GEO. The two strategies remain complementary, not interchangeable.

Frequently asked questions about SEO services

What is organic SEO?

Organic search, or SEO (Search Engine Optimization), covers all the techniques that help your website rank higher in the natural (unpaid) results of search engines like Google. The goal: getting found by the people searching for your services.

How long before I see SEO results?

SEO is a medium- and long-term investment. The first results usually show after a few months, but the visibility you earn is lasting and keeps working for you over time, unlike advertising, which stops the moment you stop investing.

SEO or Google Ads: which should I choose?

The two complement each other. Google Ads gives you immediate visibility, while SEO builds lasting visibility. Good SEO also makes your Google Ads campaigns perform better. Combining both is often ideal.

Can you guarantee the first position on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee the first position, because Google alone decides its rankings based on hundreds of criteria. What we do guarantee is rigorous work based on best practices and a measurable improvement in your visibility.

Do I need to rebuild my website to improve my SEO?

Not necessarily. We can work with your current website. That said, if your site is very old or poorly structured, a redesign can sometimes be the most cost-effective solution. We will tell you honestly after our analysis.
