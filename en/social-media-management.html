Frequently asked questions about social media management

Which social networks do you manage?

We manage the main networks based on your clientele: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, plus any other platforms relevant to your industry. We advise you on the most strategic networks for your business rather than being everywhere for no reason.

Do you create the content or do I provide it?

We create, plan and publish the content for you. We can start from your material (photos, information, promotions) or produce content entirely on our side, always staying true to your brand.

How often will you publish?

Frequency is set according to your plan and your goals. We favour consistency and quality over quantity, with a publishing calendar planned in advance.

Will I have to handle day-to-day management?

No, that's exactly what sets you free: we handle creation, planning and publishing. You focus on your business while we fuel your community.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. As with all our services, we work without long-term commitments. Results and a relationship of trust are what keep us together. Discover our approach.
