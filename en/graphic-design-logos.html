Frequently asked questions about graphic design & logos

Do you create logos from scratch?

Yes. We create fully custom logos, designed to reflect your business's personality and set you apart. We can also refresh an existing logo while preserving what makes it yours.

What types of visuals can you produce?

Logos, ad banners, social media graphics, and a variety of print or digital marketing materials. Everything your business needs for a professional, consistent image.

Will I receive my logo in every format?

Yes. We deliver your logo in all the formats needed for web and print, so it stays sharp and usable everywhere, for every occasion.

Is graphic design useful for my ads and website?

Absolutely. A strong, consistent visual identity boosts the impact of your website, your Google Ads campaigns and your social media posts. It all works together: that's what makes your brand recognizable.

Can I request changes?

Of course. We work with you, listening to your feedback, until the result looks like you and delights you. The client relationship is at the heart of how we work.
