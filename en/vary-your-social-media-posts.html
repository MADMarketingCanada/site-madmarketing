Vary your social media posts!

Varying the types of posts you publish on social media is essential to keep your audience interested and engaged. Here are some post ideas to consider:

  • Educational content: share articles, infographics or tutorials to inform your audience about topics relevant to your industry.
  • Entertaining content: publish funny videos, memes or lighthearted stories to entertain your audience.
  • News and updates: keep your audience informed of the latest news, company updates or industry developments.
  • Questions and polls: engage your audience by asking questions or running polls to gather their opinions.
  • Contests and giveaways: run contests or giveaways to spark interaction and grow your brand's visibility.
  • Testimonials and references: share client testimonials, case studies or references to strengthen your credibility.
  • Behind the scenes (BTS): offer a peek behind the curtain to humanize your brand and bond with your audience.
  • Products and services in action: show your products or services at work through demos, tutorials or explainer videos.
  • User-generated content (UGC): highlight content created by your customers by sharing it on your platforms, building trust and community.
  • Inspiring quotes: share inspiring quotes or motivating messages to spark positive emotions.
  • Weekly or monthly recaps: sum up your company's highlights, achievements or events.
  • Live events: stream events, interviews or Q&A sessions for real-time interaction.
  • Collaborations and partnerships: announce collaborations with other businesses or influencers to widen your audience.
  • Challenges: launch challenges or dedicated hashtags to encourage your community to participate.
  • Frequently asked questions (FAQ): address the questions your audience asks most, providing useful information.
  • Tips and tricks: share practical tips your audience can benefit from.
